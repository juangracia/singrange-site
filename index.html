SingRange

Know Your Voice

Find your vocal range in real time, discover your voice type, and see which artists and songs you can actually sing.

What it does

  • Detects your lowest and highest notes live, using your device's microphone.
  • Classifies your voice type, from bass to soprano.
  • Matches you to artists and songs that sit in your range.
  • Tracks your range over time and lets you share your results.

Private by design

Audio is analyzed live on your device and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. SingRange has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.
