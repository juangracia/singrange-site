Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 30, 2026

SingRange is developed by Juan Gracia. The short version: SingRange does not collect, store, or share any personal data, and nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

Microphone

SingRange uses your device's microphone to detect the pitch of your voice in real time. This is the core function of the app.

  • Audio is analyzed live, on your device, to estimate the musical note you are singing.
  • Audio is never recorded, never saved, and never transmitted anywhere.
  • No audio data is sent to us or to any third party.

You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device's Settings. The pitch features will stop working, but the rest of the app remains usable.

Information stored on your device

SingRange saves a small amount of data locally on your device so the app remembers your activity and settings:

  • Your vocal range test results and history.
  • Your preferences (theme, language, reference pitch, notation, and similar settings).

This information is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes this data.

Information we do not collect

SingRange does not:

  • Collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.
  • Use accounts or require sign-in.
  • Include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party software development kits.
  • Make network requests to send your data anywhere.

Children's privacy

SingRange does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
