SingRange

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 19, 2026

SingRange is developed by Juan Gracia. The short version: SingRange has no accounts and never records or transmits your voice. The only thing that leaves your device is anonymous, aggregate usage analytics that help us improve the app. It is never linked to your identity, never sold, and never used to track you across other apps or websites.

Microphone

SingRange uses your device's microphone to detect the pitch of your voice in real time. This is the core function of the app.

  • Audio is analyzed live, on your device, to estimate the musical note you are singing.
  • Audio is never recorded, never saved, and never transmitted anywhere.
  • No audio data is sent to us or to any third party.

You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device's Settings. The pitch features will stop working, but the rest of the app remains usable.

Information stored on your device

SingRange saves a small amount of data locally on your device so the app remembers your activity and settings:

  • Your vocal range test results and history.
  • Your preferences (theme, language, reference pitch, notation, and similar settings).

This information is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes this data.

Anonymous usage analytics

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, SingRange records a small amount of anonymous, aggregate usage data using PostHog (processed on servers in the European Union). This covers product-interaction events such as which screens you open, when you start or finish a vocal range test, sharing a result, and changing a setting, together with your app version, platform, and the language/region reported by your device.

  • It is tied only to a random, app-generated identifier, not to your name, email, account, or any real-world identity (SingRange has none of these).
  • It is not used to track you across other apps or websites, and is never sold or shared for advertising.
  • It never includes your audio, your microphone input, or your saved range results.
  • Session recording is disabled.

Information we do not collect

SingRange does not:

  • Collect personal information such as your name, email address, or precise location.
  • Use accounts or require sign-in.
  • Record, store, or transmit your voice or microphone audio.
  • Show advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.

Children's privacy

SingRange does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage analytics described above contain no personal information. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
